<div class="i18n-en" markdown="1">

I am an incoming PhD student at the University of Warwick, starting in October 2026. I am currently completing an MSc in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Southampton, where my dissertation is supervised by Prof. Tony Bagnall.

My research interests lie in machine learning for neural and physiological signals, with a current focus on EEG: parameter-efficient tuning of large models on small, noisy recordings (LoRA-One and related low-rank methods), and semantic and emotion decoding from brain signals in the direction opened up by frameworks such as DeWave. Running through this is an interest in what a benchmark number actually measures — my [MSc dissertation](/research/) found that on the SEED-VII emotion dataset, a classifier seeing nothing but video clip duration scores 67.5% under leave-one-subject-out, well above the real EEG and eye-tracking models. I have also worked on lightweight transformer architectures for 3D medical image segmentation.
